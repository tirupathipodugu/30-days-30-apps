How to play

Escape the black hole

  1. The black hole is pulling you in. You are the small glowing bubble next to it.
  2. TapClick or press Space as fast as you can. Every tap pushes you toward where you tap. Slow tapping is not enough: aim for 4 taps a second.
  3. Dodge the pieces falling in. If one hits you, it drags you toward the hole. Steer around it.
  4. Reach the "Save the universe" button on the other side to win.

Or choose another universe
