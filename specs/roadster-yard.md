# Day 5: Roadster Thruster Yard

The first input was not a written spec. It was a YouTube video.

## Prompt 1

> I will give you a youtube video. can you build a threejs game which exactly shows whats in the video. Its tesla vehicle. I want you to build as realistic car as possible.

> https://www.youtube.com/shorts/FjmY-pziC4U  
> its a futuristic flying car. But don't overcomplicate it. instead just do whatever there in the video. first replicate that using three.js then i will tell you what you should do for game.

The AI opened the video, stepped through it frame by frame, and rebuilt the scene: the night set, the curved concrete ramp with red LED edges, the steel truss tower, the drive up the curl, the inverted lip, the flip on thrusters, and the landing in smoke.

## Follow-ups that shaped the game

- "it has to be damn realistic" + "check if there are any open source tesla car models": swapped the code-built car for an openly licensed 2nd-gen Roadster model (CC BY 4.0), compressed from 30 MB to 2.4 MB.
- A second video, https://www.youtube.com/shorts/sMRkS-YYrxQ: the Semi, Cybertruck and Model Y line-up, the truss gate, power lines and the G-force HUD.
- "It has to be glassy with reflection. Make a mini game where user can control this car in an open space. Put obstacles. Give smoother options to control the booster, and quick actions like the two videos with one click."
- "make the car shiny and roads as well", then a Real Racing 3 screenshot as the bar for realism: daylight from a real HDRI sky, a matching sun and soft contact shadows.
- "I want multiplayer controls like yesterday's game. Multiple people can join by selecting their colour of choice."
