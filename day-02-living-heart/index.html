    How to explore

    • ← → rotate, ↑ ↓ tilt. On a phone, drag with one finger.
    • + − (or W S, Shift+↑ ↓, the mouse wheel, or a pinch) zoom. Keep zooming in to fly inside the heart.
    • Click or tap any part of the heart to learn what it does. Fly to takes you there.
    • Cutaway slices the heart open; Tour follows the blood through it, one stop at a time.
    • Click a glass thread to block it with a clot, and click the clot to clear it. Watch how the heart copes.
