Turn the world into blocks.

Point your camera and walls, floor, sofa, plants and you become blocks, live. Freeze it to explore, break and move things. It all runs on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

Drag to look around. Pinch or scroll to zoom.

Laptop: click to look around, WASD to walk, space to jump, left click breaks, right click places, 1 to 9 picks a block, Esc stops. Phone: left thumb walks, drag to look, tap breaks, hold places.
